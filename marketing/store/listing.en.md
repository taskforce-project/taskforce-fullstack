## Product name

TaskForce

## Description

TaskForce brings your projects, your tasks and your notes together in one app, on your own computer.

Plan: projects, tasks and a roadmap on one board.
Hand over: give a task to Claude Code, which works on your computer while you keep the control.
Remember: the Brain OS links your notes to each other and to your projects.

Your data stays with you. Your projects live on your computer, the app sends no usage data, and it works offline after
the first sign-in.

Pick your AI. TaskForce works with Ollama on your computer, or with an API key from OpenAI, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter,
LM Studio or any OpenAI-compatible service. Everything except the AI features works without a model.

Bring your work along. If you used the TaskForce web app, sign in with the same account and copy your workspaces, with
their projects and notes, in a few clicks.

Free and open source, under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license: https://github.com/taskforce-project/taskforce-fullstack

Good to know
- A free TaskForce account is needed to sign in the first time, in your browser.
- Handing tasks to Claude Code needs Claude Code installed and signed in with your own account.
- The app keeps its data in its own folder on this computer: uninstalling the app deletes it.
- 8 GB of memory is enough for TaskForce itself; a local AI model needs more.
