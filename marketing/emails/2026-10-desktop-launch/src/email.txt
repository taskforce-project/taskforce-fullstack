PLAN IT, HAND IT OVER, KEEP WHAT YOU LEARN

- Plan on a board: projects, issues, cycles, a roadmap and a backlog.
- Hand it to Claude Code: pick Claude Code as the assignee; it works on your computer and reports back.
- Keep it in the Brain OS: pages linked to each other and to your projects.


MADE FOR THE WORK YOU DO EVERY DAY

One place for the work. Your projects, their issues, cycles and pages, and a dashboard with your queue and what changed recently.

Help when you want it. Claude Code takes an issue on your computer and opens a pull request when the project has a GitHub repository. The assistant runs on a local model through Ollama, or on your own API key.

Yours. Your projects stay on your computer, the app sends no usage data, and it keeps working offline once you have signed in.


INSTALLED IN THREE STEPS (WINDOWS)

1. Download. Get the Windows installer from the download page (TaskForce-Setup-1.2.1.exe today, about 270 MB): https://taskforce-project.fr/download
2. Install. Open it: it installs for your account, without administrator rights, then opens TaskForce. After that, updates download by themselves and install when you restart it.
3. Sign in. TaskForce opens your browser: sign in with your usual account, by email, Google or GitHub, or create a free one. Then you are in.

If Windows hesitates: the installer is not code-signed yet (a certificate is a paid subscription this free project does not take), so Windows or your antivirus may stop it the first time. Each file is scanned by dozens of antivirus engines first:
VirusTotal report: https://www.virustotal.com/gui/file/fbde7957158d715ba9b626e6056cd466da49f00af149d4b72f0a33e3a46a79c0
The install guide explains each message, SmartScreen, Smart App Control and quarantine: https://taskforce-project.fr/download#install


BRING IT ALONG, OR START FRESH

I already use TaskForce
Sign in with the same account. In the app, open Operations, click From the web app, then Copy everything. Each workspace arrives with its Brain OS and its projects: issues, comments, history, cycles, pages and attachments.
It is a copy: the web app keeps your work until it closes. The date is not set yet, and you will get an email before.
To redo by hand: teammates (the app is for one person), GitHub and your other connections, and your AI settings.
Moving from the web app: https://taskforce-project.fr/migrate

I'm starting fresh
Install, sign in or create your free account, and go through a three-step setup. TaskForce opens on your own workspace: create a project and add your first issues.
Want Claude Code to pick up issues? Connect it in one click in Settings, Agents. It needs Claude Code installed and signed in with your own account.
Changed your mind later? A whole workspace goes back to the web app from Settings, General, while it is open.
